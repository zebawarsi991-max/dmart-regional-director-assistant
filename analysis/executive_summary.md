Executive Summary

Header:

Dataset: Store Daily Operations (data/store_daily_clean.csv)

Period Covered: 90 days (April 2026 to June 2026)

Author: Regional Director Analytics Assistant

Headline Finding:
Network revenue performance is heavily polarized, with Mumbai outperforming target expectations while Pune suffers from chronic target underachievement driven by low customer footfall rather than inventory stockouts.

Magnitude with Numbers:
Mumbai leads the network with a mean daily revenue of 1064371.60 INR (101.37 percent target achievement and 0 Poor band days). In contrast, Pune lags significantly at 636358.55 INR daily revenue (70.71 percent target achievement), spending 70 out of 90 days in the Poor performance band.

What Is Driving It and What Is Not:
Revenue is driven primarily by customer footfall volume, showing a strong positive correlation of 0.9182 overall. Pune underperformance is not driven by stockout duration; Pune ranks fourth in mean stockout hours at 2.32 hours, whereas Hyderabad records the highest stockouts at 3.85 hours.

Supporting Facts:

Mumbai achieved highest daily revenue (1064371.60 INR) with lowest stockout hours (1.93 hours).

Pune recorded lowest daily footfall (1517.71) compared to network average (2023.59).

Revenue per footfall efficiency remains stable across stores (Pune 419.29 INR vs overall 425.75 INR).

Open Hypotheses:

Pune lower footfall stems from local demographic density and competitor proximity (Unconfirmed; needs local footfall traffic analysis and competitor location maps).

Reducing Hyderabad stockouts below 2 hours will boost target achievement by 5 percent (Unconfirmed; needs item-level lost-sales logs).

Recommendation:
The Regional Supply Chain Manager must audit stockout replenishment cycles in Hyderabad within 14 days, while the Commercial Operations Manager must reassess Pune sales targets and local marketing spend within 30 days.
